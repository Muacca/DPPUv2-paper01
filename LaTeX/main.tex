\begin{abstract}
We compare the Einstein--Cartan (EC) action with a constant complete
Nieh--Yan (NY) coupling restricted to static homogeneous geometries on
$S^3\times S^1$, $T^3\times S^1$, and compact
$\mathrm{Nil}^3\times S^1$.
A metric-compatible connection carries axial and vector torsion.
Specifying the connection and local invariants separately from lattices,
periods, and integration volumes fixes the global normalization of each
local calculation.

On the ansatz, the two contributions to the complete NY density coincide:
$T^a\wedge T_a-e^a\wedge e^b\wedge R_{ab}=0$.
The reduced potential of the complete action is therefore independent
of the NY coupling $\theta$.
In the one-variable classification with torsion coefficients and circle
circumference fixed, $S^3$ has a strict radius minimum without a barrier
for $V\ne0$, $|\eta|<2$.
The complete action has no strict positive-radius minimum on isotropic
or fixed-shape torus expansion, or on uniform Nil expansion.
Varying one torus radius with the other two fixed is a distinct problem,
whose minimum condition is also stated.

Diagnostic actions adding only the torsion-side or positive curvature-side
NY density coincide on the ansatz. They can have barrier-separated minima
from the mixed term, but these are not results for the complete NY action.
The classification follows analytically from cubic polynomials, with
boundaries, degeneracies, and numerical limitations distinguished.

These are off-shell classifications along fixed variation paths.
A radius minimum does not ensure stationarity under full variations
including the independent connection, torsion, and circle circumference,
Lorentzian dynamical stability, or physical masses and decay rates.
For standard source-free EC gravity without a cosmological constant
and with a constant NY coupling, a separate nonexistence obstruction
applies to smooth nondegenerate full vacuum solutions on closed real
Euclidean $S^3\times S^1$.
\end{abstract}
